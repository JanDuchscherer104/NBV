# ARIA-NBV scaffold five-PR rebuild context

## Decision

Replace the checkpoint's mixed scaffold-history validator with one narrow OMX
artifact lifecycle gate. Current accepted evidence stays in native role paths;
only superseded evidence is archived.

## Boundaries

- The accepted report is preserved byte-for-byte at its native specification path.
- The accepted Prometheus plan changes only its runtime-identifier status line
  and the requested external-pin wording and links.
- The two pre-policy plans are represented only by a privacy-safe disposition
  record; their unredacted payloads are removed from the active tree.
- Raw transcript data, runtime state, and Graphify output are outside this
  bundle; aggregate history, path, LOC, and evidence-coverage ledgers are
  registered specifications.

## Acceptance basis

The current bundle contains all six role families, including exactly one hashed
acceptance-record specification. The registry binds the bundle to its baseline
commit and binds artifact provenance by native path, byte count, and SHA-256.
