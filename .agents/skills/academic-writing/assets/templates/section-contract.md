# Section Contract

Before drafting a substantial section, record the reader question, one to three
scoped claims, supporting source identities, limitations, destination, and the
verification needed before realization. Keep this task-local: active thesis
sources, bibliography, code, tests, and report artifacts remain authoritative.

For every claim, name its scope, evidence strength, falsifier, and the reason a
skeptical reader should accept its current wording. A missing source, result,
or contract is an explicit gap rather than prose to smooth over.
