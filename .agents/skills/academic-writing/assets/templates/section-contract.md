# Section Contract

Before drafting a substantial section, record:

- incoming reader state and reader question;
- one to three scoped claims and durable takeaways;
- supporting source identities and limitations;
- outgoing dependency: what the reader can understand or assess next;
- destination, main-text or appendix status, and verification before realization.

Keep this task-local: active thesis sources, bibliography, code, tests, and
report artifacts remain authoritative.

For every claim, name its scope, evidence strength, falsifier, and the reason a
skeptical reader should accept its current wording. A missing source, result,
or contract is an explicit gap rather than prose to smooth over.
