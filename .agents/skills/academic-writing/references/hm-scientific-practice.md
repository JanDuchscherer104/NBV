# HM Scientific-Practice Overlay

Checked 2026-08-25. Read this branch for an HM/FK07 thesis, assessment, or
release review that presents scientific methods, data, software, or results.
Refresh the official sources at freeze time; this reference is an operational
checklist, not a submission receipt.

## Authority Boundary

- [HM ASPO §20](https://mediapool.hm.edu/media/dachmarke/dm_transfer/download_13/aspo_2/ASPO_iF_05_AES.pdf)
  makes good scientific practice binding for written assessment work.
- ASPO §26(7) and the
  [FK07 thesis process](https://cs.hm.edu/studierende/abschlussarbeiten.de.html)
  own the thesis-declaration clauses. Realize those clauses in the exact Typst
  declaration owner rather than duplicating them here.
- The [HM statute on good scientific practice](https://mediapool.hm.edu/media/dachmarke/dm_transfer/diehochschulemnchen/satzungen/dokumente2010/satzung_gwp/00_Satzung_GWP.pdf)
  applies to people scientifically active at HM and gives the institution's
  detailed research-process standard. Its examples expressly include faculty,
  research/support staff, and doctoral researchers; confirm direct student
  applicability when it affects a legal or submission decision.

## Research Process

Apply the HM statute's §§7-8 as an integrity and quality-assurance check:

- choose traceable, scientifically grounded methods and describe the type and
  extent of research data produced;
- record quality-assurance mechanisms throughout collection, processing,
  analysis, software selection, development, and programming;
- preserve a route by which another researcher can inspect or replicate the
  result;
- exclude fabrication, falsification, selective rejection of unwanted results,
  and misleading manipulation of figures or data;
- address conscious and unconscious bias and state whether sex, gender, or
  diversity considerations are relevant to the research question.

## Documentation And Retention

Apply §11 proportionately to the thesis evidence package:

- retain the information needed to inspect and assess each result, including
  the research process, methods, quality assurance, and individual results that
  do not support the hypothesis;
- protect documentation from manipulation;
- plan retention for publicly released results, data, central materials, and
  relevant software; the statute normally specifies ten years from publication;
- document a traceable reason when the expected documentation or archiving
  itself cannot occur. Restricted public access does not by itself justify
  omitting the retained evidence record.

## Access, Software, And AI Programs

Apply §12 without turning openness into an absolute promise:

- make underlying data, methods, materials, software, and documentation
  accessible where possible, or identify their provenance and the substitute
  verification route;
- cite original sources and record third-party rights, privacy, contractual,
  patent, or security constraints that limit access;
- keep publicly accessible source code persistent, citable, and documented;
- publish self-written software with source and an appropriate license when the
  statute applies and no documented exception controls;
- label programs created with AI software as such when applicable, while the
  author retains responsibility for verification and disclosure.

## Completion Check

The HM branch is complete when the thesis claim points to inspectable evidence,
methods and quality assurance are documented, non-supporting outcomes remain
visible, retention and access have an explicit route or justified exception,
software provenance and licensing are clear, and the current official source
versions have been rechecked.
