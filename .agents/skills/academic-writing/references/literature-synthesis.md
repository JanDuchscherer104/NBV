# Literature synthesis

Read this branch for Related Work or other concept-centered synthesis.

Organize each paragraph around a conceptual tension rather than a catalogue:

1. name the subfield or problem;
2. state what selected primary sources contribute;
3. compare assumptions, evidence, or limitations;
4. identify the bounded unresolved tension relevant to the section; and
5. retain source identities and exact locators beside resulting claims.

Use the repository's literature and bibliography owners and inspect the cited
primary source before making a literature claim. Reviews can frame a field but
do not replace primary support. A missing source is a provenance gap; mark it
or narrow the sentence.
