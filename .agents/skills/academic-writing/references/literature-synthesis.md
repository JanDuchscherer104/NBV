# Literature synthesis

Read this branch for Related Work or other concept-centred synthesis.

Teach the conceptual machinery that makes the later method understandable.
Organize each unit as:

`problem or tension -> approach family -> comparison dimension -> established result -> unresolved limitation -> thesis consequence`

Compare assumptions, inductive biases, evidence, and limitations rather than
cataloguing papers or architectures. Order concepts by what the reader must
understand first, and retain citation identities and exact locators beside the
resulting claims.

Use the repository literature and bibliography owners and inspect the cited
primary source before making a literature claim. Reviews can frame a field but
do not replace primary support. A missing source is a provenance gap; narrow the
sentence or mark the gap.
