# Upstream Matt Writing Modes For ARIA Academic Writing

Use this as reference-only inspiration for `academic-writing` prose work. Do not
activate raw Matt writing skills for ARIA-NBV thesis writing.

## Upstream Sources

The reviewed upstream files and revision are registered as
`matt-writing-modes` in `.agents/skill-sources.toml`:

- `skills/in-progress/writing-shape/SKILL.md`

The grounding ID is provenance only and never activates upstream maintenance.

## ARIA Adaptation

- Scratch files are not thesis truth. They may hold fragments, examples,
  objections, and phrases, but final prose belongs in active Typst thesis
  sources only after claim, citation, notation, and section-contract checks.
- Grounding maps to ARIA glossary and notation discipline: introduce durable
  terms through `docs/typst/shared` owners before relying on them in final prose.
- Final prose must pass the local paragraph unit test: one job, one scoped
  claim, evidence path, terminology consistency, transition, and explicit
  uncertainty.
- Missing evidence stays visible as a gap, hypothesis, limitation, or planned
  experiment. Do not let narrative flow hide unsupported claims.
- Shape and beat mechanics are subordinate to ARIA section contracts. They can
  improve coherence and concision, but they cannot change what a thesis section
  is allowed to claim.
