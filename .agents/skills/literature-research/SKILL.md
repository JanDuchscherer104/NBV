# Literature Research

Prepare a bounded, source-traceable research packet. This optional lane owns
discovery and screening, not argument construction, scientific certification,
or academic phase state.

## Workflow

1. Read `docs/AGENTS.md`, then the current
   [`sources.jsonl`](../../../docs/literature/sources.jsonl),
   [`references.bib`](../../../docs/references.bib), and the smallest matching
   review under [`docs/contents/literature/`](../../../docs/contents/literature/).
2. Read [`workflow.md`](references/workflow.md). Define the question, inclusion
   boundary, comparison dimensions, and either `research-only` or explicitly
   authorized `research-and-integrate` mode before collecting sources.
3. Keep exact identities, primary-source locators, roles, assumptions,
   limitations, and conflicting evidence beside each selected item. Field
   summaries can guide discovery but cannot support a retained claim.
4. Hand the packet to `academic-writing` for argument construction. Send
   disputed validity or overclaiming concerns to `scientific-review`.

## Completion

The task-local packet states the question, screened scope, selected source
identities, conceptual dependencies, comparison dimensions, assumptions,
limitations, disconfirming evidence, gaps, and next owner. Any authorized
canonical-record edit follows `docs/AGENTS.md`; the packet itself does not
duplicate those records or decide the final narrative.
