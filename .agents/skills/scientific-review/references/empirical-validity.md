# Empirical validity checks

Read only the checks named by the review question. Inspect exact evidence
owners and record gaps instead of inferring a pass.

- **Leakage:** actor-visible inputs, oracle information, splits, and labels do
  not cross the declared boundary.
- **Confounding:** treatment, baseline, ablation, and comparison families
  isolate the stated factor; uncontrolled differences remain findings.
- **Units and uncertainty:** experimental units, aggregation, denominators,
  variability, and missingness match the estimand.
- **Parity and escalation:** baselines, compute, calibration, and evaluation
  protocol are comparable; pilot or exploratory evidence is not promoted to a
  confirmatory claim.
- **Freshness:** source revision, report identity, environment, and artifacts
  resolve to the reviewed candidate.

These checks establish findings and evidence gaps, not scientific validity,
novelty, or release approval.
