# Independent review protocol

1. Freeze the candidate identity, scope, and review question.
2. Choose applicable validity checks and inspect exact code, data,
   configuration, source, and report owners rather than summaries.
3. Record every finding as `severity`, `evidence`, `reason`, and `action`.
   Distinguish a confirmed defect, evidence gap, and author question.
4. Return findings with reviewer provenance and candidate identity. The owner
   decides whether and how to mutate, then may request a fresh review.

Review proves neither entailment nor novelty; it exposes risks for owner-led
resolution.
