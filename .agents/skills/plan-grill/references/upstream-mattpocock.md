Matt grilling skills as ARIA defaults.

- one material decision at a time when decisions are dependent;
- a recommended answer plus the tradeoff before asking;
- concrete scenario probes for fuzzy language;
- terminology challenges when the user's wording is overloaded.

  `.agents/references/source_order.md`, not a generic `docs/adr/` or legacy
  state-journal destination.
