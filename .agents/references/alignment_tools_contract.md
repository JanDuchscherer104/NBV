# Alignment Tools Contract

Optional operator tools produce navigation, evidence, diagnostics, or proposals.
They never become ARIA-NBV truth owners merely because they are convenient,
recent, or provenance-aware.

## Tool Boundaries

- OMX may orchestrate work and preserve explicitly accepted planning evidence.
  Runtime state, drafts, logs, sessions, and generated reports remain local.
- Graphify may navigate source-derived code, thesis, and literature structure.
  Exact sources remain authoritative, and scaffold/configuration lookup uses
  direct repository inspection.
- MemPalace may retrieve historical context and temporal assertions. It is not a
  truth engine; contradiction support is incomplete and experimental, and a
  source backlink is not formal provenance.
- Matt skills and other external skills are optional operator-local workflow
  inputs. Their presence does not change repository ownership.
- External research, MCP, browser, visual, or autoresearch systems may emit
  evidence and suggested updates. Durable changes go through the owner selected
  by `.agents/references/source_order.md`.

## Autoresearch Adapter

Autoresearch remains replaceable. Measured autoresearch may alternate research
and executable measurement, but its outputs remain evidence or proposals until
the exact owner is reviewed and updated.

## Visual And UI Gates

Visual and UI inspection produces evidence. Durable findings belong in the
owning code, tests, measurements, narrative, preference, or backlog surface.

## Promotion Rule

Tool output is promoted only by a reviewed edit to the exact owner. Promotion
records identify the source hash, observed scope/time, reviewer, conflicts,
supersession, and resulting owner commit or artifact hash. Similarity alone
never establishes contradiction, acceptance, or supersession.

Every optional-tool route must retain a direct-source fallback. No adapter may
write policy, backlog, scientific narrative, human preferences, or accepted OMX
state implicitly.
